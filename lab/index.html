Agents pursue their nearest detectable prey. Each dot is one agent. Pursuit supports up to one million agents. Contact battles use the CPU contact order and support up to 1,024 agents with solid walls. Outbreak contacts model bites and incubation. The main arena includes the full AI.

Massive mode also uses 360° perception when creatures are smaller than a pixel. Exact pursuit keeps the same rules at every zoom.
